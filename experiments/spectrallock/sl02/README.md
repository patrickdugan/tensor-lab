# SpectralLock-02: causal cross-shell mixing and split-algebra proof gadget

2026-11-27. Synthetic research only. No new secure primitive, proof-system integration, real-key attack, hardware, GPU or Fable.

Prime-power shell transform F_j=P_(j-1)-ell^(j-1)s_j, P_j=P_(j-1)+(ell-1)ell^(j-1)s_j; output ordered F_1..F_n,DC. For q prime >4N, N=ell^n, any exact F_j has at most two binary prefixes. For ell>2 the prefix weights are superincreasing; for ell2 the first two weights coincide and later weights double; interior sums have at most two witnesses, extreme sums one. The two shifted ranges meet only at zero, whose extreme witnesses also number two.

Each fresh independent uniform public row mixing F_j and earlier coordinates at deadline j yields collision probability at most beta=(1+2*(ceil(q/B)-1))/q for any prefix with different F_j. Conditioning on earlier rows preserves independence. Thus E K_j <=2+2 beta E K_(j-1) for every covered deadline, with K_w<=2^(w+1) after w unconstrained steps. For B8, q>4N, 2beta<1 uniformly. Sum geometric recurrence: expected O(n+2^w) attempted extensions when all later deadlines include F_j. Retain full histories for mixed observations. Dense row evaluation adds O(n) operations per test; no bit-cost or worst-case claim. Globally coupled sparse rows need not satisfy the coverage hypothesis.

The natural integer shell transform T obeys T²=N I and T(a*b)=Ta elementwise*Tb. These integer polynomial identities hold by reduction over arbitrary coefficient rings; if N is a unit, T is a bijective algebra homomorphism to a product of n+1 copies of the coefficient ring. No primitive Nth root required in that ring. Tensor CRT extends to known coprime prime-power factors. Explains the diagonal structure of spectral rounding of shell convolution; says nothing about general time-coordinate rounding or standard Ring/Module-LWR. A truly uniform dense public matrix remains uniform under fixed invertible transforms, so ordinary LWR remains a separate distribution. 2910 direct convolution coordinate and 140 involution checks including composite rings.

R1CS over prime field q coprime to N: for each a,b,c, alias P0=s0, introduce P_j,F_j with two linear equations per j (linear form times 1 equals zero). Enforce F^a_j F^b_j=F^c_j and P^a_n P^b_n=P^c_n. Exactly 6n linear, n+1 nonlinear, 7n+1 total rows, 1+3(n+1)+6n variables including fixed constant. Recurrence forces the spectra; invertibility proves the field convolution relation and all correct products admit witnesses. At d1024, 1024 multiplication rows and 7162 total. Schoolbook d² products is a baseline, not a lower bound. 112 positive witnesses,2516 corrupted output/auxiliary/constant wires rejected,4608 direct convolution checks.
